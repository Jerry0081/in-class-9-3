Section 2: Creative Coding Artist——Camille Utterback

Camille Utterback:

She is an artist who uses motion detectors for her projects and let the audience movements and gestures reflects on the screen.

Her interactive concept can be fully applied to the immersive theater that I am interested in. Especially her well-known work where people use their bodies to catch the falling letters, this can be regarded as a hidden Easter egg within the scene, or it can be used to guide the audience to interact.
